At the default operating point, the proposed IRS-Opt controller reaches 20.92 dB average SNR, 13.771 bps/Hz sum rate, and 9.500 bps/Hz secrecy rate with a robust-gain metric of 12.251. Relative to the legitimate-only AO baseline, the proposed method delivers -0.99\% higher sum rate and 13.95\% higher secrecy rate, while maintaining 1.67\% outage probability at the 5 dB operating point. At this operating point, the proposed controller also exceeds the greedy baseline in secrecy by 13.76\%, which supports a clean secrecy-first positioning. The direct-link baseline remains substantially weaker, with secrecy dropping from 9.500 bps/Hz to 6.736 bps/Hz under the same setting. Across the tested CSI-error range, the proposed method preserves a positive secrecy advantage over greedy between 13.67\% and 37.26\%, which is the cleaner robustness claim to emphasize in the final manuscript. The solver uses 6 projected updates over 4 robust channel samples, whereas the AO baseline uses 3 legitimate-only updates without secrecy coupling.
