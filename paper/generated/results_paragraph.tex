At the default operating point, the proposed IRS-Opt controller reaches 23.81 dB average SNR, 15.373 bps/Hz sum rate, and 10.439 bps/Hz secrecy rate with a robust-gain metric of 13.631. Relative to the legitimate-only AO baseline, the proposed method delivers -1.09\% higher sum rate and 12.67\% higher secrecy rate, while maintaining 1.11\% outage probability at the 5 dB operating point. At this operating point, the proposed controller also exceeds the greedy baseline in secrecy by 15.17\%, which supports a clean secrecy-first positioning. The direct-link baseline remains substantially weaker, with secrecy dropping from 10.439 bps/Hz to 7.483 bps/Hz under the same setting. Across the tested CSI-error range, the proposed method preserves a positive secrecy advantage over greedy between 15.08\% and 32.99\%, which is the cleaner robustness claim to emphasize in the final manuscript. The solver uses 10 projected updates over 8 robust channel samples, whereas the AO baseline uses 5 legitimate-only updates without secrecy coupling.
