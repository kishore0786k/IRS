# IEEE Submission Checklist

This checklist is meant to be completed before submitting the manuscript to an IEEE journal.

## Packaging

- Confirm the manuscript uses the current IEEE journal article format.
- Confirm all figures are legible in grayscale and high-resolution output.
- Confirm table titles, figure captions, and equation notation are consistent.
- Confirm author names, affiliations, funding, and acknowledgments are complete.

## Research Integrity

- Confirm all reported data and simulation settings are accurate.
- Confirm the submission is original and not plagiarized.
- Confirm every figure, table, and claim is traceable to generated artifacts in this repo.
- Confirm all coauthors meet IEEE authorship criteria and approved the submitted version.

## Technical Content

- Confirm the related-work section and bibliography match the final target journal style.
- State the novelty claim in one paragraph and support it with baseline comparisons.
- Report parameter settings, random seed, Monte Carlo budget, and convergence evidence.
- Check that the final claims match the generated tables and figures exactly.

## Reproducibility

- Regenerate the publication package with the final chosen `--mc` value.
- Archive `results/publication_package/publication_package.json` with the submission materials.
- Archive `results/publication_package/submission_readiness.md` and `paper/reference_inventory.md`.
- Keep the final figure PDFs from `paper/figures/generated/`.
- Re-read `results/publication_package/publication_summary.md` before final submission.
