At the default operating point, the proposed IRS-Opt controller reaches 23.86 dB average SNR, 15.416 bps/Hz sum rate, and 10.526 bps/Hz secrecy rate with a robust-gain metric of 13.680. Relative to the legitimate-only AO baseline, the proposed method delivers -1.19\% higher sum rate and 13.26\% higher secrecy rate, while maintaining 1.67\% outage probability at the 5 dB operating point. At this operating point, the proposed controller also exceeds the greedy baseline in secrecy by 13.86\%, which supports a clean secrecy-first positioning. The direct-link baseline remains substantially weaker, with secrecy dropping from 10.526 bps/Hz to 7.492 bps/Hz under the same setting. Across the tested CSI-error range, the proposed method preserves a positive secrecy advantage over greedy between 13.81\% and 34.46\%, which is the cleaner robustness claim to emphasize in the final manuscript. The solver uses 6 projected updates over 4 robust channel samples, whereas the AO baseline uses 3 legitimate-only updates without secrecy coupling.
